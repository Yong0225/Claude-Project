Heritage,
served daily
from first light to last cup

Tucked into The Veranda on Jalan Burma, The Heritage Collection is Daily Coffee’s all-day dining room. Mornings begin with specialty coffee and a quiet table, lunch brings the Malaysian classics Penang grew up on, and evenings slow down with continental plates beneath old brick walls and arched windows.
